# Industry practice: the economics team (2026-11-21)

The question: what does an economics (macro) team do at a quant firm and at a fundamental firm?

**Quant firms against fundamental firms.** At a quant firm, macro is mostly a risk factor or a state
variable. AQR finds that the time variation of factor premia "appears unrelated to macroeconomic
risks", and that factor timing gives "modest predictability that likely fails to overcome implementation
costs" (Ilmanen, Israel, Lee, Moskowitz and Thapar, "How Do Factor Premia Vary Over Time? A Century of Evidence", JOIM 19(4), 2021, aqr.com) [read]. Man Group uses the similarity of today to past
dates on 7 market variables (Mulliner et al., "Regimes", JPM 52(4), 2026; man.com/insights/regimes-systematic-models-power-of-prediction) [summary]. At a fundamental
firm, macro is a top-down overlay (country, sector, theme) and the rate and premium context of the DCF.

**Usual errors.** (1) Look-ahead from revised data (Croushore and Stark, Philadelphia Fed WP 00-6)
[summary]. (2) Overconfidence: forecasters missed the size of recessions until the year was almost
over (An, Jalles and Loungani, IMF WP/18/39, 2018, imf.org) [summary]. (3) A futures path read as a forecast:
the prices contain risk premia (Piazzesi and Swanson, JME 55(4), 2008, nber.org/papers/w10547) [summary]. (4) A rule that fails
out of sample (the Sahm rule triggered in July 2024 with no recession; Fortune, 2024-07-29, fortune.com) [summary]. (5) Signals that come
too late: the NBER dates a peak months after it occurs (nber.org/research/business-cycle-dating) [read].

## What we do the same

- A supporting role: the briefs give context and set no model input (`chiefs/ORIENTATION.md`, section 1).
- Market-implied paths are the price of the market, not our forecast (agent check 4).
- The rate and spread context of a valuation, for the Head of Fundamental Research.

## What we do not do

- We have no vintage (ALFRED) series and no labor, GDP, PCE or financial-conditions series in the store.
- We do not use the published models (NY Fed term-spread probability, NFCI, Sahm rule) as a standard table.
- We have no scenario set or list of stress dates for the backtests of the Head of Quant Research.

## Proposed changes (at most three)

1. **Data request to the Head of Technology:** FRED series `UNRATE`, `PAYEMS`, `GDPC1`, `PCEPILFE`,
   `NFCI`, `SAHMREALTIME`, `T10Y3M`, `VIXCLS`, and their ALFRED vintages where a test uses history.
   Cost: one data-engineer run, a FRED key, and a check of the terms for a public copy.
2. **Agent file `macro-economist.md`:** give a published model with each cycle reading, and the vintage
   of a revised series (or "latest values, not real-time"). Cost: two lines of the agent file.
3. **Offer to the Head of Quant Research:** one list of stress dates and one scenario set (the Fed format,
   fewer variables), for the backtests. Only if that head wants it. Cost: one macro-economist run for the
   dates, one more for the scenario set.
