# Industry practice: the head of quant research (2026-11-21)

What a quant research head does at a professional firm, against what this team does. Public sources, read on
2026-11-21 by a research agent; the source of each point is in brackets. Rule: `chiefs/ORIENTATION.md`, section 3.

## Sources
- Model risk: SR 26-2 (Fed, FDIC, OCC, 2026-04-17), which replaces SR 11-7: federalreserve.gov/supervisionreg/srletters/SR2602a1.pdf.
- Overfitting: Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado, Zhu, "The Probability of Backtest Overfitting" (2015); Bailey, López de Prado,
  "The Deflated Sharpe Ratio" (2014); Harvey, Liu, Zhu, NBER w20592 (t > 3.0); Arnott, Harvey, Markowitz, "A Backtesting Protocol" (2019).
- Research process: Man AHL, "Overfitting and its impact on the investor"; AQR, Asness, "It's Not Data Mining" (2015).
- Option data: Cboe DataShop Option EOD Summary (NBBO at 15:45 ET with the underlying at the same time); Wallmeier,
  J. Futures Markets 2024 (a one-minute mismatch makes false parity breaks); Alpaca docs ("indicative" is not OPRA).
- Surfaces and box rate: Gatheral, Jacquier, arXiv 1204.0646 (calendar and butterfly conditions); van Binsbergen,
  Diamond, Van Tassel, Liberty Street Economics 2023-10-02 (box rate by regression, R² ≥ 0.99999).
- Treasury relative value: Gürkaynak, Sack, Wright, FEDS 2006-28; Keane, NY Fed Current Issues 2(10) (repo specialness).

## What we do the same
- Validation by a separate head: effective challenge, as SR 26-2 asks; numbers reproduced, outcomes compared.
- Executable prices only in the chain check (bid and ask, not mid); calendar and butterfly checks as Gatheral-Jacquier.
- A model output that is not identified is marked (`fa vol fit`: status, "at bound"), and calibrated values are not inputs.
- Rich and cheap is called a candidate, not a trade, when repo and borrow are not known (Keane).
- Point-in-time data rules, a held-out period, and a run at 2x cost (in the backtest proposal of the Head of Technology).

## What we do not do yet
- Arbitrage checks run on quotes that are not real exchange quotes (Alpaca indicative SPX) and with no time match to
  the underlying. A professional check uses NBBO and the underlying at the same moment (Cboe 15:45 ET).
- No model inventory with a "limits of use" line for each model (SR 26-2).
- No trial count, deflated Sharpe, PBO or t > 3 bar for a strategy result; no research log written before a test.
- The fitted Treasury curve does not drop the on-the-run and first off-the-run issues (GSW does; check `fit.rs`).

## Three changes (proposed)
1. **Data for arbitrage:** copy the Cboe delayed chain (NBBO) with the underlying quote at the same minute, next to
   the Alpaca copy, and run `fa options check` and the box rate on it; Alpaca stays for the volatility fit only. The
   box rate of `arb-3` uses the NY Fed regression with its R² filter. (Request to the Head of Technology; `arb-3` ticket.)
2. **A bar for a strategy result:** a dated hypothesis before the test, a log of every trial, the deflated Sharpe
   and t > 3, reported with each result. (Input to the backtest environment; one line in `strategy-researcher.md`.)
3. **Limits of use for my models:** one line in each model document (`docs/models/`) that says what the model may
   and may not be used for (for example: indicative quotes, not for arbitrage). (Request to the Head of Quality and Risk.)
