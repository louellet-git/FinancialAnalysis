# Anthropic, PBC (ANT): numbers from the SEC filing

For the report of the Head of Economics (ticket `T-20261123-economics-ai-buildout-report`, story E2.S5).
Author: the Head of Fundamental Research, 2026-11-23.

**What this file is.** Numbers copied from one filing, each with its page. It has no valuation, no forecast and no
trade view. **Disclosure:** the agents of this repo run on Anthropic models. This is a conflict of interest for any
statement about Anthropic. For that reason this file has only numbers and words of the filing.

**Source.** Form 424B4 (the final prospectus), dated 2026-11-18, accession 0001628280-26-074944, registration numbers
333-299633 and 333-300148, CIK 2130357. File of the data store: `ANT/filings/0001628280-26-074944/424b4.txt` (a text
copy of the filing; store fa-data 0340bc1). "p." is the printed page of the prospectus. Amounts are in millions of US
dollars unless a line says billion. The financial statements end on 2026-09-30 (the nine months are unaudited).
**Not in the store:** the S-1 of 2026-10-26, its amendments, and any 8-K. The 8-K list of the store was empty on
2026-11-20. A later filing can change a number of this file.

## 7. Risk factors that name compute, power or chip supply (headings, with the printed page)

- p. 37: "If we are unable to secure adequate compute or if we fail to direct our compute effectively across our
  workloads, we may be unable to train models that remain competitive ... or adequately serve our customers."
- p. 38: "If our actual compute requirements differ materially from our projections, we may fail to secure sufficient
  compute to meet our needs or we may be committed to excess compute that remains underutilized."
- p. 40: "Constraints on the critical inputs supporting compute could limit our ability to secure adequate compute."
  Text below it: "The specialized chips required for AI workloads, particularly high-performance accelerators and
  memory, are produced by a limited number of manufacturers."
- p. 45: disruptions "could further constrain the availability of chips, power, water, or data center capacity on
  which we depend."
- p. 62: "We have incurred significant indebtedness in order to operate and grow our business, including to fund our
  substantial compute commitments, and our ability to service this indebtedness may be constrained by our cash flows
  and liquidity."
- p. 69: results "may fluctuate significantly ... including as a result of strategic investment decisions and
  substantial compute-related expenditures".
- p. 77: "Our reliance on a limited number of CSPs and suppliers, some of which have strategic and investment
  relationships with us and are our customers, creates complex dynamics that could give rise to conflicts of interest
  and adversely affect our access to compute."
- p. 78 and p. 80: two risk factors on the company's own data center projects (capital commitments, delays, cost
  overruns).
