# Uranium Price Needed: NexGen

*For NexGen, a uranium miner in the URA fund: the uranium price at which the value of the company equals its stock price. Prices of 2026-11-20.*

**The question.** You asked why URA, a fund of uranium miners, is at its level. The stock price of a miner shows the
uranium price that investors expect. So we ask: at which uranium price does the value of a miner equal its stock price?
Today we can answer for one miner only, not for the fund: NexGen (6.1% of URA on 2026-07-31, the fourth largest holding).

**The answer.** The value of NexGen equals its stock price (US$6.40) at a uranium price of about **US$84 a pound**
(range US$80 to US$85). On 2026-10-30 a pound of uranium was at US$91.55 for delivery now (the spot price) and at
US$98.00 in long-term contracts.

**What it means.** Investors pay for NexGen as if uranium stays at about US$84, which is 8% below the spot price.
They do not pay for a rise of the uranium price. This is consistent with a small discount for a mine that is not
built yet; we did not test that reason. The number covers one company. It does not show that NexGen or URA is cheap or
expensive, because the mine value is the company's own estimate. It is not a trade view.

**How we know**

- NexGen has one mine project (Rook I, 100% owned). It got its licence in March 2026 and construction started in June.
- In its report for the third quarter of 2026, the company prints what the mine is worth at 8 uranium prices.
- We add the cash and the uranium that the company holds, and we subtract its debt. Then we compare the total with
  the price of all its shares on the stock market.
- At US$80 the company is worth less than the market pays. At US$90 it is worth more. The two are equal at about US$84.

**Table 1: What NexGen is worth at US$80 and at US$90 a pound, from the company's own table** (millions of Canadian dollars).
See: the market pays an amount between the two totals.

| Part | Uranium at US$80 | Uranium at US$90 |
|---|---:|---:|
| The Rook I mine: its value after tax, from the company's table | 4,890 | 5,840 |
| The uranium that the company holds (2.7 million pounds) | 307 | 346 |
| Cash and short-term investments | 822 | 822 |
| Its 27.8% of the company IsoEnergy | 181 | 181 |
| Less its debt | -356 | -356 |
| **Total: what the company is worth** | **5,844** | **6,833** |
| **What the market pays**: 688.0 million shares at US$6.40 | **6,267** | **6,267** |

**How sure the number is**

- The range is US$80 to US$85. It changes with the exchange rate, with the date of the debt number, and with the part
  of the building cost that the company paid already.
- US$84 can be too high: we give no value to the uranium in the ground that is not in the mine plan.
- US$84 can be too low: the company's table has no delay and no building risk. The company also says that it needs
  more money to complete the mine.

**Why only NexGen.** We opened the reports of four URA miners.

that print no table (Cameco is the largest), and it gives a range, not one fixed price. This is the miner model of the
