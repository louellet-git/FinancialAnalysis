**What the week shows**

- **The fall is common.** Each of the 10 fell. The required return rose for 68 of 68 S&P 100 companies with a value.
- **The sector ranks the falls.** Chips -17.8% (7 of 7 companies). Computers, chips and software -11.9%. The other
  sectors of the S&P 100 about -6.4%. Six of the 10 are in the first sector: thus the 10 fell more than the market.
- **Hit more: NVDA.** Its fall is consistent with its sector: MU (-23.6%) and AMD (-20.5%) fell as much. Against the
  other chips NVDA lost 5.9 points more (1.5 deviations), most of it on 11-18, the day after its results, and on 11-20.
- **Hit less: AAPL.** It fell 3.8% where its beta gives about 7%: 1.0 deviation above the line. META is 1.0 below.
  In a usual week about 3 of 10 stocks are that far, so these two are of the usual size. BRK-B and LLY fell little
  because their betas are low (0.7).
- **Two phases.** Monday to Thursday was the full market (equal weight -4.4%): the falls rank by beta, not by
  sensitivity to rates. Friday was the chips (-11.0%) while the 10-year yield fell 12 basis points.
