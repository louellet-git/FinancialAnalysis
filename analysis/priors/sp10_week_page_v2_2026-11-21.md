*Week of 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-20 (5 days): which days, which stocks, and the reason that the market gives.*

The 10 largest companies fell 9.96%. All 10 fell, and the sector of a stock gives the size of its fall. The largest day
was Friday, and it was a fall of the chip companies. The press gives a doubt about the AI build-out as the reason, and
our data is consistent with it. No stock of the 10 is far from its sector.

Against the market: the change less beta (measured against SPY) times the equal-weight S&P 500, in weekly deviations (104 weeks).
In a usual week about 3 of 10 stocks are 1 deviation or more from the line (normal distribution).

**What the week shows**

- **The fall is common.** Each of the 10 fell, and the market fell on each of the 5 days.
- **The sector gives the size.** Chips -17.8% (7 of 7 companies of the S&P 100). Computers, chips and software -11.9%.
  The other sectors about -6.4%. Six of the 10 are in the first sector: thus the 10 fell more than the market.
- **Hit more: NVDA.** It is 2.7 deviations below the market line. That is consistent with its sector: MU fell more
  (-23.6%) and AMD fell 20.5%. Against the other chips NVDA lost 5.9 points more, which is 1.1 deviations: a usual size.
- **Hit less: AAPL.** It fell 3.8% where its beta gives about 7%: 1.0 deviation above the line, a usual size. META is
  1.0 below. BRK-B and LLY fell little because their betas are low (0.7).

**The time after** (the Head of Economics, with the implied growth of our engine)

- The chip prices still need a revenue growth of about 25% to 30% each year for 10 years. MSFT needs about 17%.
- The week took about 4 points from the needed growth of NVDA and MU, and 1 to 2 points from that of the buyers.
- The market expects less from the AI build-out, but still much. A further fall needs a cut of real orders.
- The next dates: the capital-spending guidance of the buyers in their next reports, and the Fed decision of 12-09.
