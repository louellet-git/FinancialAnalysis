# S&P 10: The Fall by Day and by Stock

*Week of 2026-11-13 to 2026-11-20 (5 days): which days, which stocks, and the reason that the market gives.*

Background: the S&P 10 is the 10 largest companies of the S&P 500 by market value. In this week the S&P 500 fund SPY
fell 8.56%. This is page 1 of 3: page 2 gives the valuation blocks, and page 3 the chip companies of the S&P 500.

The 10 largest companies fell 9.96%. All 10 fell, and the size of each fall goes with the sector of the stock. The press
gives one reason through the week: a doubt that the AI build-out will pay for its cost and its debt. Our data is
consistent with it on each day, most of all on Friday, the day of the chip companies. A rate rise is not supported.

Against the market: the change less beta (measured against SPY) times the equal-weight S&P 500, in weekly deviations (104 weeks).
In a usual week about 3 of 10 stocks are 1 deviation or more from the line (normal distribution).

**What the week shows**

- **The size goes with the sector** (S&P 100, by market value). Chips -17.8% (7 of 7 companies). Computers, chips and
  software -11.9%. The other sectors about -6.4%. Six of the 10 are in the first sector: thus the 10 fell more than the market.
- **Hit more: NVDA.** It is 2.7 deviations below the market line. That is consistent with its sector: MU fell more
  (-23.6%) and AMD fell 20.5%. Against the other chips NVDA lost 5.9 points more, which is 1.1 deviations: a usual size.
- **Hit less: AAPL.** It fell 3.8% where its beta gives about 7%: 1.0 deviation above the line, a usual size. META is
  1.0 below. BRK-B and LLY fell little, which is consistent with their low betas (0.7).

**The time after** (the Head of Economics; the growth numbers are those of page 2)

- The chip prices still need a revenue growth of 25% to 29% each year for 10 years (MU, AVGO, NVDA). MSFT needs 16%.
- The week took 4.2 points from the needed growth of NVDA and 4.9 from MU. It took 1.7 to 2.4 points from the buyers.
- The market expects less from the AI build-out than before, but it still expects much.
- The next dates: the capital-spending guidance of the buyers in their next reports, and the Fed decision of 12-09.
