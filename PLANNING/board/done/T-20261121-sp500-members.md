OUT of the public store. Each head fetches it into its local cache with one command. The MIT licence of the repo
covers the code, not the right to redistribute the base file (from a book); index membership itself is a public fact,
so local use for research is acceptable.

the book Trading Evolved (Clenow); the later part is from the Wikipedia S&P 500 page (CC BY-SA).
